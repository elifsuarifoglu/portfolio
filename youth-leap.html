The Project

Building better ways for young people to participate.

Youth Leadership and Empowerment Through Active Participation (YOUTH LEAP) is an Erasmus+ project focused on developing sustainable models for youth participation.

The project builds on the youth participation model developed by Başkent Gençlik Meclisi (Capital Youth Assembly) and explores how such structures can be adapted and shared across European contexts.

For me, the most interesting part has been seeing how a local youth participation idea can become a framework for international collaboration.

Youth participation is not simply about giving young people a voice. It is about creating structures where that voice can lead to action.

My Role

Participating in a real international collaboration.

I participated as an active delegate within the Youth LEAP framework, working alongside young people from partner countries and engaging with the project's youth participation model.

My experience involved international meetings, collaborative discussions, civic participation activities, and learning how youth organizations can structure decision-making in inclusive ways.

What interested me most The project connected something very local — student and youth participation — with a much larger European conversation about democracy and civic engagement.
Project Objectives

Designing participation, not just discussing it.

Youth LEAP focuses on practical structures that make youth participation possible.

01 · COLLABORATION

International Cooperation

Developing and sharing youth participation practices across partner countries while working from common democratic principles.

02 · PRACTICE

Field-Based Participation

Turning youth participation from an abstract concept into a practical framework informed by real activities and collaboration.

03 · STRUCTURE

Youth Councils

Supporting the development of youth councils and participation structures in partner countries.

04 · MENTORSHIP

Reverse Mentorship

Encouraging young people to learn from and mentor each other rather than relying only on traditional top-down structures.

Youth Participation Model

A structure designed around participation.

The model associated with Başkent Gençlik Meclisi is based on inclusive and relatively horizontal participation, allowing young people to contribute according to their interests and areas of expertise.

Within the model, different commissions and committees create spaces for young people to work on specific topics while remaining connected to a larger civic structure.

6 Commissions International Cooperation · Culture & Arts · Youth Policies · Environment & Urban Rights · GNC & New Technologies · Psychosocial
4 Committees Gender Equality · Accessibility · Sports · Technova
Key Activities

From meetings to simulations.

The project combined international exchange with practical experiences in youth participation and civic engagement.

01 · INTERNATIONAL

Partner Meetings

International meetings brought together delegates and youth leaders from partner countries to exchange experiences and develop the participation model.

02 · SIMULATION

Model City Council

A simulation brought together 147 young people in commissions to discuss issues, develop proposals, and practice civic decision-making.

03 · PARTICIPATION

Youth Gatherings

Local, national, and international youth gatherings created opportunities to discuss youth participation and amplify young voices.

What I Learned

Participation needs architecture.

Youth LEAP changed how I think about civic participation. It is easy to say that young people should have a voice, but meaningful participation requires structures that make contribution possible. Working across countries also showed me that the same model can look different depending on the community using it. I became more interested in how institutions, technology, and people can work together to make participation more than a slogan.

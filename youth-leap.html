Youth LEAP

Erasmus+ · International

About Youth LEAP

Youth Leadership and Empowerment Through Active Participation (YOUTH LEAP) is an Erasmus+ project that brings together young leaders from across Europe to develop sustainable youth participation models. The project is built on the foundation of Başkent Gençlik Meclisi's (Capital Youth Assembly) innovative youth participation framework.

🇪🇺 European Impact
The Youth Participation Model of Başkent Gençlik Meclisi is now being implemented in Italy, Spain, and Bulgaria, creating a democratic youth engagement framework at the EU level.
🇹🇷 Turkey 🇮🇹 Italy 🇪🇸 Spain 🇧🇬 Bulgaria

🎯 Project Objectives

  • 🤝
    International Collaboration: Establishing a youth participation model based on democratic values across European Union countries.
  • 📋
    Field-Based Model: Creating a practical, field-tested youth participation framework through collaboration with academic partners at METU.
  • 🏛️
    Youth Councils: Enabling the establishment of youth councils in Bulgaria, Italy, and Spain through this model.
  • 🧠
    Reverse Mentorship: Implementing a system where young people lead and mentor each other, breaking traditional hierarchical structures.

📌 Key Activities

  • ✈️
    International Meetings: Delegates from Başkent Gençlik Meclisi traveled to Italy to meet with youth leaders from partner countries, sharing experiences and co-creating the participation model.
  • 🏛️
    Model City Council Simulation: 147 young people from different ideologies come together in commission to produce policies, submit proposals, and advocate for real-world implementation.
  • 📢
    Youth Participation Activities: Organizing local, national, and international youth gatherings to amplify youth voices in decision-making processes.

🌱 The Youth Participation Model

The model developed through YOUTH LEAP is built on inclusive, horizontal hierarchy principles. Young people aged 15-35 can join without any restrictions—regardless of ideology, background, or political affiliation.

Six Commissions and Four Committees allow members to work in their areas of interest:

  • 🌍
    Commissions: International Cooperation, Culture & Arts, Youth Policies, Environment & Urban Rights, GNC & New Technologies, Psychosocial
  • 🎯
    Committees: Gender Equality, Accessibility, Sports, Technova (for engineering students)

Semicolon

I could have been a period, bringing an end, A comma, too, silently waiting to descend. But I remained between—neither stop nor cease, A semicolon: pausing, yet finding release. I could have written an end to the dark of the night, Yet a sliver of light crept in—my sentence took flight. For in every story they thought was done, They say a new beginning has just begun. Whatever broke me, whatever tore me apart, Was etched not on my skin, but deep in my heart. But look—here I stand, still breathing, still here, With my half-finished sentences, unbroken, sincere. You thought it was over, didn't you? Words fell silent, roads blurred from view, But my story didn't stop at that door— Because I did not choose to end with a period's floor. Like breath, like a wave, like a storm at sea, I fell and rose, was wounded, yet free. Still I walked, still I wrote through the night— Because I did not choose to fade with a comma's slight. At times I was quiet, at times I would cry, My knees would tremble, my heart would go dry. But I came to see—my road has no final bend, My story is not over; this is not the end.

Sky Cemetery

While night takes the hours as its own, Stars are born like a dream overthrown. With the whispers of souls in the air, Like screams made of light, beyond compare. Graves no longer lie upon the ground— Hearts are buried in the skyward bound. The heavens have become a vast graveyard, Capturing everyone, keeping them barred. The planets no longer turn in place, They only flee from each other's face. No death wishes to touch another kind— For if they did, the order would unwind. At the edge of a galaxy far away, There is a silence whose name I cannot say. There, guards keep watch without rest— No hands, no eyes, yet they perceive my quest; With every step I take, they follow my trace. Inside my chest, a star pounds every night, Like a forgotten dead one, out of sight, Like a love that no longer remembers me... And you... Your star is fading, slowly, quietly, Because in this vast, unending sea, There is no one left who remembers you. But I am still alive. Though I do not remember you, Still, every night I look up to the sky, Watching the stars drift by. And without knowing, I celebrate the triumph Of having forgotten you.
Published

Comma

I think I am truly half now— I've become like a comma, Forgotten, unable to carry on. I was dragged from place to place, Waited for the period to end it all. The semicolon turned out to be a lie— It left me behind mercilessly. Life crossed out my sentences one last time, Words were brushed aside meaninglessly with me. I was forgotten, I couldn't explain— The fault is mine, of course, for those who understand. Among these empty words, My story remained unfinished, Like a comma scattered into life's lines. I wished to stay for a moment, I tried— How surprising, isn't it? I couldn't succeed. Amid all this chaos, I was forgotten—I couldn't remain, Couldn't hold on— I was sent off into nothingness, like a comma.
Published

Reyyan

We became less one morning— The sun that rose with your eyes had set. When you were still a child, They forgot your childhood, it seems… You fell silent, and the world went deaf. Was your heart already tired at fourteen? Or was the world too heavy on your shoulders? There was a scream inside you— No one heard you, not even I. Now I ask the stars for your name, The sky gives no answer, the night is cold. But in every star, I see you still— It hurts to look, and I remember once more. I wish I had held your hands that day, Held you a little tighter, closer still. Maybe even silence with you would have been enough— But I was too late, as I always am. There is no more pain now— This world can no longer make you cry. But I will always miss you— In every new dawn, in every lost star…
Published

Love in Ankara

A streetlamp flickers before my eyes, The misty sky hangs heavy overhead. No one knows—and if they do, they stay silent— Ankara, loneliness suits you instead. Old memories pass through the intersections, In my hand, a forgotten warmth remains. Cold pavements sometimes speak to me, A teary glance falls from my eyes like rain. With every step into this grey city, Every thought dissolves into the wind. There is a void inside you, in Ankara— A night resembling a dream you've sinned. Cigarette smoke wraps around the air, I hear your voice—but no one is near. Hope in my eyes, coldness in my chest, Love in Ankara is always hidden, never clear. Old trees tremble in the empty park, Every corner, every wall leaves a trace. Those with broken hearts in Ankara, Remain with disappointment, like the love they embrace. Sorrow in my eyes, wind in the street, Every step I take is searching for you. Every pain, every fracture lived in Ankara, Cannot be forgotten—my heart bears a wound that's true. Then everything stops, time slips away, The grey city falls silent, I forget you, they say. But every corner, every street, every night, Leaves a mark—reminding me of you anyway.
Published

Cinderella

And the hours are passing by, Cinderella's fairy tale is running dry. The cycle breaks apart, Everyone returns to where they belong—back to the start. Don't run, Cinderella— This ending was always clear. The fairies have gone now, It's time to go home, my dear.

You

You never once turned to look at me, My name was unknown in your world. You passed by me like the wind, Your silence wounded my soul without mercy. Your name never leaves my lips, But if asked, I couldn't speak of its pain. Your eyes looked at me like I was nothing— Always cold, always distant, your breath. The poems I wrote for you, The verses I chose so carefully, Couldn't even be crumpled up like nothing— They never reached you. You never saw me, you never knew me, You passed right through my heart. But I, every night, with your name on my lips, Kept vanishing into your existence.

The Paradox of Loss

A weak star glides from my depths within, A piece of life falls from where I've been. The fox turns back, my shadow starts to shake, Each glance of hers tears my heart, makes it ache. Whispers sink deep into my skin, Silence hides beneath my fingertips, within. Every memory a wound, an old scar left behind, With every breath, hope dissolves, unkind. Nights are long, dreams are blurred, My memories stay distant, unheard. Glass is sharp, my heart is broken, The fox collects my pieces, leaves them smoking. Silent fish, lost reverie, Bind every broken piece of you to the fox, you see. Foxes wander through her mind, Their tails tangle in seaweed, though they try to unwind. The fish drifts through cold waters wide— Won't it perish in this eternal tide? Yet the fish still searches for the fox again, But what does a fox seek in the ocean's domain? Foolish fish, drifting, struggling to survive, The fox smirks slyly, grinning alive. His heart still lingers on her shore, While hers belongs to no one—silent, wanting more. And the night ends, but the dream goes on, Drown in the ocean, live on the lawn— Fish and fox, an endless game, The seaweed still burns for her, death remains the same.

I Am Waiting

I ask questions into the void. Silence… no answer. Are your eyes now just a memory, Or a dream that never truly was? Does he love me, or not? Oh daisy, Are you also just a game of a dream? They said you would return. But you were never truly mine— So what does "returning" even mean? Perhaps you are lost inside me, Vanished within my very being… I am waiting. Perhaps for a moment, perhaps for a trace, Perhaps just for a single word. Something—a sign from you. And if I am still here, Perhaps it is only to see: Is this real, or is it a play? This time, let *you* decide. And I— I am still waiting. Truthfully, for you— But most of all, for the things I could not even confess to myself.

Mimozia

A psychological thriller visual novel where storytelling, programming, exploration, and player choice become one system.

I created Mimozia to explore what happens when a story is no longer something you simply read, but something you navigate, influence, and uncover.

Ren'Py Python Interactive Fiction Narrative Design
The Project

A story designed to be explored.

Mimozia is a psychological thriller visual novel developed with the Ren'Py engine.

The player follows Özgür, a young man who travels to a mysterious town searching for his missing girlfriend, Elif. His investigation leads him to the abandoned Star Hospital and a secret experiment known as Mimozia.

The project combines narrative design with programming: choices change character statistics, unlock dialogue, reveal clues, and ultimately influence the ending.

Why I Made It

I wanted to make storytelling interactive.

I have always been interested in storytelling, but writing a linear story only gives the reader one path through the world.

With Mimozia, I wanted to experiment with what changes when the audience becomes part of the structure itself.

Programming allowed me to turn narrative decisions into systems: relationships could change, information could be discovered in different orders, and the same story could end in different ways.

Mimozia became a way to use code not just to build a game, but to build a different kind of story.
The Story

A missing girl. An abandoned hospital. A hidden experiment.

Three months after Elif disappears, Özgür arrives in the isolated town where she was last seen.

With no money and nowhere to stay, Özgür finds work as a night watchman at Star Hospital, an abandoned building filled with unanswered questions.

As he explores the hospital, he discovers evidence that Elif was connected to a secret experiment called Mimozia, a project designed to transfer psychic abilities between subjects.

The deeper Özgür goes, the harder it becomes to distinguish what is real, what is memory, and what the experiment has changed.

Systems Design

The story is powered by systems.

Instead of treating the game as a collection of scenes, I designed systems that connect player decisions to the narrative.

01 · BRANCHING

Choices & Endings

Player decisions affect relationships, dialogue, available information, and eventually the ending of the story.

02 · STATS

Character State

Logic, Trust, and Psychic Sensitivity evolve throughout the game and unlock different interactions.

03 · EXPLORATION

World Navigation

Players move through multiple floors and rooms, discovering clues and tracking their progress through the hospital.

04 · INFORMATION

Dual-Perspective Notes

Notes from Özgür and Elif provide different perspectives, gradually revealing the larger mystery.

05 · PUZZLES

Investigation

Number sequences, code cracking, and symbol matching connect exploration with problem solving.

06 · CONSEQUENCE

Player Agency

Information is not simply presented to the player. What they discover and how they act changes their path through the game.

Technical Work

Code became part of the storytelling.

Mimozia uses Ren'Py's scripting system together with Python to manage branching dialogue, character state, exploration logic, puzzles, note collection, and multiple endings.

The challenge was not only writing scenes. It was making sure the different systems could interact without breaking the narrative structure.

Ren'Py Python Branching Logic State Management Interactive Fiction Narrative Design
What I Learned

Writing and programming solve different problems — and become interesting when they work together.

Mimozia taught me to think of narrative as a system. A scene is not only something the player sees; it can be a consequence of everything they did before it. Building that structure made me more interested in the space between creative writing and engineering, where rules can become a form of storytelling.

Current Status

Still in development.

Mimozia is a personal project that continues to evolve. I plan to release the completed game on Itch.io.

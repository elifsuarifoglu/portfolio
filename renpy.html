Mimozia

Python · Narrative · Interactive

About the Game

Mimozia is an interactive psychological thriller visual novel developed using the Ren'Py engine. The game follows Özgür, a young man who travels to a mysterious town to find his missing girlfriend, Elif — only to uncover a dark secret hidden within the walls of an abandoned hospital.

🎮 Game Overview
A psychological thriller visual novel with multiple endings, branching narratives, and a deep mystery involving a secret project called "Mimozia".

📖 Story

Three months after Elif's sudden disappearance, Özgür arrives at a small, isolated town where she was last seen. With no money and nowhere to stay, he finds a job as a night watchman at the abandoned Eski Yıldız Hastanesi (Old Star Hospital).

As Özgür explores the hospital's dark corridors, he discovers that Elif was part of a secret experiment called "Mimozia" — a project designed to transfer psychic abilities between subjects. But something went terribly wrong, and now Mimozia has awakened inside Elif.

🔍 Key Features

🧠 Branching Narrative

Your choices matter — every decision affects Özgür's stats (Mantik, Güven, Psişik Duyarlılık) and leads to different endings.

🔎 Exploration

Search through 30+ rooms across 6 floors of the hospital, each containing clues about the Mimozia project.

📝 Note System

Collect notes from Özgür and Elif's perspectives, building a complete picture of the mystery.

🎭 Multiple Endings

4 different endings based on your choices: Mükemmel Son, Duygusal Son, Mantıksal Son, Trajik Son.

🧩 Puzzles

Solve intricate puzzles using clues found throughout the hospital to unlock hidden areas.

🌌 Psychological Thriller

A gripping story inspired by classic psychological horror with a sci-fi twist.

🛠️ Technologies Used

🐍 Ren'Py 📖 Python 🎨 Visual Novel 🧠 Psychological Thriller 🔎 Interactive Fiction

🎯 Game Systems

  • 📊
    Stat System: Three core stats — Mantik (Logic), Güven (Trust), and Psişik Duyarlılık (Psychic Sensitivity) — evolve based on your choices and unlock unique dialogue options.
  • 🗺️
    Exploration System: Navigate through multiple floors of the hospital, each with unique rooms and discoveries. Track your progress with the exploration map.
  • 📝
    Note Collection: Discover notes from both Özgür and Elif's perspectives. New notes are highlighted with a "+" indicator for easy tracking.
  • 🧩
    Puzzle System: Solve cryptographic puzzles (number sequences, code cracking, symbol matching) to unlock hidden areas and advance the story.
  • 🎭
    Multiple Endings: Your choices determine the final outcome. Will you save Elif, or will Mimozia consume everything?

📜 Development

Mimozia was developed as a personal project to explore interactive storytelling and the Ren'Py engine. The game features a complex branching narrative with over 100+ unique scenes, 30+ explorable locations, and a deeply layered mystery that unfolds through exploration and investigation.

The game is currently in development and will be available for download on Itch.io upon completion.

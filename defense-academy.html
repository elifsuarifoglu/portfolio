Defense Industry Academy

SSB · National Competence Initiative

About the Program

The Savunma Lise Eğitim Programı (Defense High School Education Program) is an initiative by the T.C. Cumhurbaşkanlığı Savunma Sanayii Başkanlığı (SSB) under the Milli Yetkinlik Hamlesi (National Competence Initiative). This 11-week program introduces high school students to Turkey's defense industry ecosystem, its historical development, and future trends[citation:1][citation:2].

🎯 Program Mission
To raise awareness about the Turkish defense industry, provide insight into its products and history, address ethics and confidentiality, and analyze current and near-future technologies[citation:1].

🏛️ Executing Institutions

T.C. Cumhurbaşkanlığı Savunma Sanayii Başkanlığı (Presidency of the Republic of Türkiye Presidency of Defence Industries) Savunma Sanayii Akademi (Defense Industry Academy) TR Eğitim ve Teknoloji A.Ş. (TR Education and Technology Inc.) T.C. Millî Eğitim Bakanlığı (Republic of Türkiye Ministry of National Education) Savunma Kariyer (Defense Career)

📚 What I Learned

  • 🔐
    Security & Defense Concepts: Understanding fundamental security and defense concepts and discovering career opportunities in the field[citation:1].
  • ⏳
    Historical Development: Examining how defense technologies have evolved from the past to the present[citation:1].
  • 🇹🇷
    Turkish Defense Industry: Learning about the dynamics of the Turkish defense industry and the opportunities it offers[citation:1].
  • 🚀
    Future Trends: Understanding where defense technologies are heading and what to expect in the coming years[citation:1].
  • ⚖️
    Ethics in Defense: Exploring the relationship between defense technologies and ethical responsibilities[citation:1].
  • 💼
    Career Opportunities: Discovering how to build a career in the defense industry sector[citation:1].

Upon successful completion of the 11-week program, participants receive an official Katılım Belgesi (Certificate of Participation) recognized by the Presidency of Defense Industries[citation:1].

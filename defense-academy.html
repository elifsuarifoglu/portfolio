Savunma Sanayii Akademi · 11-Week Program

Defense
Industry Academy

An intensive program that introduced me to defense technologies, security, ethics, autonomous systems, and the future of engineering.

Through the Savunma Lise Eğitim Programı, I explored Türkiye's defense technology ecosystem through weekly sessions led by experts from leading institutions.

11 Weeks Technology Engineering Ethics
The Program

Seeing engineering beyond the classroom.

The Savunma Lise Eğitim Programı is part of the Milli Yetkinlik Hamlesi of the Presidency of Defence Industries of the Republic of Türkiye.

Over 11 weeks, the program introduced high school students to the history, technologies, institutions, and future direction of Türkiye's defense industry.

For me, the value of the program was not simply learning about defense products. It was seeing how fields such as software, cybersecurity, autonomous systems, materials, AI, and human-machine interaction meet in real engineering environments.

A question I kept coming back to: How do we design powerful technologies while thinking seriously about security, responsibility, and the people who use them?

What I Explored

Eleven sessions, many different systems.

The sessions covered a surprisingly broad range of technologies and questions.

01 · SECURITY

Cybersecurity & OT Security

Explored software-based security and operational technology security, including the importance of protecting connected systems.

02 · AUTONOMY

Unmanned Systems

Learned about unmanned and autonomous platforms and the engineering challenges behind increasingly independent systems.

03 · HUMAN + MACHINE

Human-Machine Collaboration

Examined how people interact with intelligent systems and why technology design cannot ignore human decision-making.

04 · BIOSECURITY

Biosecurity

Explored the relationship between biological systems, technological development, security, and responsibility.

05 · MAPPING

Real-Time Systems

Investigated real-time mapping and dynamic environments as examples of systems that must process changing information continuously.

06 · MATERIALS

New Materials

Learned how advances in materials can create new possibilities for security and engineering applications.

07 · ENVIRONMENT

Environment & Energy

Explored how energy and environmental considerations intersect with technological systems.

08 · XR

Extended & Augmented Reality

Examined how immersive interfaces can be applied to security and operational environments.

What I Learned

Technology and responsibility cannot be separated.

The program expanded the way I think about engineering. Before encountering many of these topics, it was easy to think about technology mainly in terms of what a system can do. The sessions made me think more about what a system should do, what happens when it fails, who controls it, and what responsibilities come with building powerful tools.

I also became more interested in the intersections between AI, autonomous systems, cybersecurity, human-machine interaction, and ethics.

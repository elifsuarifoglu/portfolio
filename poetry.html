Semicolon

I could have been a period, bringing an end,
A comma, too, silently waiting to descend.
But I remained between—neither stop nor cease,
A semicolon: pausing, yet finding release.

I could have written an end to the dark of the night,
Yet a sliver of light crept in—my sentence took flight.
For in every story they thought was done,
They say a new beginning has just begun.

Whatever broke me, whatever tore me apart,
Was etched not on my skin, but deep in my heart.
But look—here I stand, still breathing, still here,
With my half-finished sentences, unbroken, sincere.

You thought it was over, didn't you?
Words fell silent, roads blurred from view,
But my story didn't stop at that door—
Because I did not choose to end with a period's floor.

Like breath, like a wave, like a storm at sea,
I fell and rose, was wounded, yet free.
Still I walked, still I wrote through the night—
Because I did not choose to fade with a comma's slight.

At times I was quiet, at times I would cry,
My knees would tremble, my heart would go dry.
But I came to see—my road has no final bend,
My story is not over; this is not the end.

Night's Conquest

While night takes the hours as its own,
Stars are born like a dream overthrown.
With the whispers of souls in the air,
Like screams made of light, beyond compare.

Graves no longer lie upon the ground—
Hearts are buried in the skyward bound.
The heavens have become a vast graveyard,
Capturing everyone, keeping them barred.

The planets no longer turn in place,
They only flee from each other's face.
No death wishes to touch another kind—
For if they did, the order would unwind.

At the edge of a galaxy far away,
There is a silence whose name I cannot say.
There, guards keep watch without rest—
No hands, no eyes, yet they perceive my quest;
With every step I take, they follow my trace.

Inside my chest, a star pounds every night,
Like a forgotten dead one, out of sight,
Like a love that no longer remembers me...

And you...
Your star is fading, slowly, quietly,
Because in this vast, unending sea,
There is no one left who remembers you.

But I am still alive.
Though I do not remember you,
Still, every night I look up to the sky,
Watching the stars drift by.

And without knowing,
I celebrate the triumph
Of having forgotten you.

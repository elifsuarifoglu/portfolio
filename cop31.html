The Context

Climate action becomes different when the conversation goes global.

IDD ORG is a platform focused on international development, diplomacy, global governance, climate action, and sustainable development.

Through this work, I have been able to move from school-level environmental initiatives into broader conversations about climate policy, youth participation, and international cooperation.

I am especially interested in the space between local action and global decision-making: how can young people make their ideas heard at both levels?

What This Changed

From environmental action to climate policy.

My work with ECOBO taught me how to organize people around environmental action within a school community.

Working through IDD ORG and the COP31 process has expanded that perspective. Climate change is not only a scientific or environmental issue; it is also a question of policy, diplomacy, communication, economics, and representation.

That intersection is what I want to explore further: how scientific knowledge becomes policy, how policy becomes action, and how young people can participate meaningfully in both.

What I Learned

Global problems need more than global conversations.

International climate work has made me think differently about impact. Speaking about climate change is important, but so are the systems underneath it: research, institutions, policy, communication, and participation. I have become more interested in how these pieces connect — and in where young people can create real leverage within them.

About IDD ORG & COP31

IDD ORG (International Development and Diplomacy Organization) is a UN-affiliated platform that brings together young leaders committed to global governance, climate action, and sustainable development. As an active delegate, I work alongside international youth to shape meaningful climate policies.

🌍 COP31 Delegate
Representing Capital Youth Assembly at COP31, contributing to pre-conference campaigns and climate policy discussions in Ankara.

📝 Key Contributions

  • ✍️
    UN-Bound Climate Magazine: Co-authored an article for a UN-affiliated climate magazine, addressing youth-led climate action and sustainable development goals.
    📖 Read the Magazine
  • 🇺🇳
    COP31 Delegation: Selected as a delegate for COP31 through Capital Youth Assembly, participating in high-level climate discussions and policy workshops.
  • 📢
    Pre-COP31 Campaigns: Organizing and leading pre-COP31 awareness campaigns in Ankara, engaging students and young professionals in climate dialogue.
  • 🌱
    Climate Advocacy: Active participation in international climate forums, advocating for inclusive and youth-centric climate policies.

🌿 Youth Leadership & Global Impact

Through IDD ORG, I have had the opportunity to collaborate with young leaders from around the world, sharing insights and strategies for effective climate governance. Our collective efforts aim to ensure that youth voices are heard in global decision-making processes, particularly in the lead-up to COP31.

The work we do through IDD ORG emphasizes the importance of international cooperation, diplomatic engagement, and actionable climate solutions that can be implemented at both local and global levels.

IDD ORG & COP31

Climate Action · Youth Advocacy · International Engagement

About IDD ORG

IDD ORG (İklim Değişmeden Değiş) is an international youth-focused organization working around climate action, environmental awareness, sustainable development, and youth engagement.

Through my involvement with IDD ORG, I have participated in climate-focused initiatives, contributed to youth advocacy, and engaged with discussions surrounding climate diplomacy and international cooperation.

🌍 COP31 DELEGATE
Selected as a youth delegate through Capital Youth Assembly, contributing to pre-COP31 engagement and climate-focused discussions in Türkiye.

📝 Key Contributions

  • ✍️
    Published Climate Writing: Co-authored a contribution for IDD ORG's climate magazine, focusing on youth, climate action, and sustainable development.
  • 🇺🇳
    COP31 Delegation: Selected as a delegate through Capital Youth Assembly and participated in climate-focused youth engagement connected to COP31.
  • 📢
    Pre-COP31 Campaigns: Contributed to awareness and engagement activities encouraging young people to participate in climate dialogue.
  • 🌱
    Climate Advocacy: Engaged with youth-centered climate discussions and initiatives focused on environmental sustainability and international cooperation.

🌿 Youth Leadership & Global Engagement

My involvement with IDD ORG has allowed me to connect with young people interested in climate action, sustainability, and international cooperation.

Through these experiences, I have explored how young people can participate not only in environmental awareness campaigns, but also in broader conversations around policy, diplomacy, and global climate governance.

🎯 What I Learned

  • 🌍
    Global Perspective: Understanding climate change as a cross-border issue requiring international cooperation.
  • 🗣️
    Advocacy: Learning how youth voices can contribute to public awareness and policy discussions.
  • 🤝
    Collaboration: Working with young people around shared environmental and social goals.
  • 📝
    Communication: Developing experience in communicating climate-related ideas through writing, campaigns, and public engagement.

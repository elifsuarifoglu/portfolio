About ECOBO

ECOBO is a student-led sustainability organization dedicated to creating a greener, more environmentally conscious school culture. As Vice President, I lead a passionate team of 60+ volunteers working on impactful projects that promote recycling, conservation, and environmental awareness.

🌍 What We Do

  • 👥
    Team Leadership: Serving as Vice President of a 60+ volunteer team, coordinating school-wide sustainability and environmental protection projects.
  • 📚
    Comprehensive Education Program: Organizing and delivering regular environmental seminars to 900+ students, raising awareness about conservation and recycling.
  • ♻️
    Efficient Waste Management: Collecting, sorting, and ensuring delivery of recyclable waste from across the school to official recycling centers.
  • 📋
    Cleanliness Motivation System: Implementing daily classroom evaluations based on cleanliness criteria, maintaining objective assessment standards.
  • 🏆
    Award and Recognition Mechanism: Rewarding the cleanest classrooms of the week and month with specially designed flags, creating visible symbols of achievement.

🎓 Special Event: Prof. Dr. Latif Kurt

We had the honor of hosting Prof. Dr. Latif Kurt, a renowned botanist and environmental scientist, for a special seminar attended by over 900 students. His inspiring talk on biodiversity, conservation, and the importance of protecting our natural heritage was a defining moment for ECOBO and our school community.

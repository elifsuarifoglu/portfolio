Beyond the projects

I’m interested in what happens when different disciplines meet.

Building JARVIS made me think about how people interact with intelligent systems. Writing Mimozia showed me how code can become a storytelling medium. ECOBO taught me that even a good idea only matters when people are willing to act on it.

Ideas are only the beginning.

I like turning them into things people can use, experience, or build on.

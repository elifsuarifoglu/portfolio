About LÖSEV

LÖSEV (Lösemili Çocuklar Vakfı) is a non-governmental organization dedicated to supporting children with leukemia and their families. As a volunteer, I actively participate in fundraising events, awareness campaigns, and community outreach programs to make a meaningful difference in the lives of these children.

💛 Mission
LÖSEV was established to ensure that every child with leukemia has access to healthcare, education, and emotional support. The organization provides free treatment services, scholarships, and psychological support to thousands of children across Turkey.

🎗️ What We Do

  • 💛
    Awareness Campaigns: Organizing and participating in campaigns to raise awareness about childhood leukemia and the importance of early diagnosis and treatment.
  • 🎯
    Fundraising Events: Actively involved in planning and executing fundraising activities to support LÖSEV's treatment and education programs.
  • 🤝
    Community Outreach: Engaging with local communities to build solidarity and support networks for children with leukemia and their families.
  • 📚
    Educational Support: Contributing to LÖSEV's scholarship programs that ensure children can continue their education during and after treatment.
  • 💪
    Emotional Support: Providing emotional support to families navigating the challenges of leukemia treatment and recovery.

💛 Why LÖSEV Matters

LÖSEV is more than just a foundation—it is a beacon of hope for thousands of families. Every child deserves a chance to grow up healthy and happy, and LÖSEV works tirelessly to ensure that children with leukemia receive the medical care, emotional support, and educational opportunities they need to thrive.

The organization's holistic approach addresses not only the medical needs of children but also their psychological well-being and academic future. By supporting LÖSEV, I am part of a community that believes in the power of solidarity and compassion to create lasting change.

LÖSEV

Volunteering with a community working to support children with leukemia and their families.

My role as a volunteer has involved taking part in awareness, fundraising, and community-oriented activities.

Volunteering Community Awareness Solidarity
The Organization

Turning solidarity into action.

LÖSEV (Lösemili Çocuklar Vakfı) is a Turkish non-governmental organization supporting children with leukemia and their families.

Its work includes healthcare-related support, education, awareness, and community initiatives for children and families affected by leukemia.

I became involved as a volunteer because I wanted to contribute to a community where even small actions can help create awareness, solidarity, and practical support.

Volunteering taught me that impact does not always begin with building something new. Sometimes it begins with showing up consistently.
My Role

Contributing where help is needed.

As a volunteer, I have participated in awareness campaigns, fundraising activities, and community outreach initiatives associated with LÖSEV.

Rather than having one fixed responsibility, volunteering has meant contributing to different activities and supporting the wider work of the organization.

What Matters

Impact is also about people.

LÖSEV has made me more aware of the human side of social impact: empathy, consistency, and community participation.

Awareness

Helping important issues become more visible within the wider community.

Solidarity

Contributing to a community built around supporting children and families.

Consistency

Learning that meaningful volunteering is often about showing up repeatedly, not just once.

What I Learned

Not every meaningful contribution has to be measurable.

My volunteering experience with LÖSEV has reminded me that social impact is not always captured by a large number or a technical achievement. Sometimes it is about awareness, participation, reliability, and being willing to contribute to work that already exists. That perspective has influenced how I think about community work alongside my more project-based experiences.
